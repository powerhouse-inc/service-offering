# Manual

Welcome to the Service Offering knowledge system. This manual explains how everything works.

This vault tracks the design, implementation, and evolution of the **Service Offering** product — a SaaS commerce system built on five document models (ServiceOffering, SubscriptionInstance, ResourceTemplate, ResourceInstance, Facet), four editors, and a GraphQL subgraph. The knowledge system captures insights about pricing structures, subscription lifecycles, resource management, and the patterns that connect them.

## Conventions Used in This Manual

- **Insights** are the atomic units of knowledge in this vault. Each insight captures one idea, pattern, or finding.
- **Maps** are navigational structures that connect related insights into coherent views.
- **Extract** means distilling raw material into insights. **Connect** means linking insights to each other. **Evolve** means updating insights as understanding deepens. **Validate** means checking that insights remain accurate and well-connected.
- Commands are shown as `/command-name`. Arguments appear in angle brackets: `/extract <source>`.
